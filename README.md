## Data and knowledge

### Open data

- [360Giving](https://www.threesixtygiving.org) - An UK charity that helps grantmakers publish and use open grants data through the 360Giving Data Standard and GrantNav. It is now part of Funders Together.
- [Centre for Public Data](https://www.centreforpublicdata.org) - The Centre for Public Data is a non-profit, non-partisan advocacy organisation working for stronger public data: data collected, published or regulated by government. It works with policymakers, parliamentarians and organisations to identify data gaps and improve legislation.
- [CoST Infrastructure Transparency Initiative](https://infrastructuretransparency.org) - CoST is a global non-profit that works with governments and civil society to publish and validate data on public infrastructure projects. It uses that data to improve transparency, accountability and participation in infrastructure investment.
- [data.gov.uk](https://www.data.gov.uk) - The UK government's public data catalogue, now being transformed into the National Data Library. It is run by GDS in the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology and still hosts the directory where public bodies publish datasets.
- [International Aid Transparency Initiative](https://iatistandard.org/en/) - The International Aid Transparency Initiative is a global initiative that publishes data on development and humanitarian spending using the IATI Standard. It provides free tools and guidance so that aid information is open and usable.
- [Office for National Statistics](https://www.ons.gov.uk) - The UK's national statistical institute and largest independent producer of official statistics. It publishes over 600 releases a year on the economy, society and population.
- [Open Data Institute](https://theodi.org) - A london-based non-profit founded in 2012 by Sir Tim Berners-Lee and Sir Nigel Shadbolt. It works on data infrastructure, research, policy, consultancy and training to build trust in data.
- [Open Data Manchester](https://www.opendatamanchester.org.uk) - Helps people, organisations and communities use data better through advice, advocacy, events, research and training.
- [Open Data Services](https://ods.coop/) - A worker-owned consultancy that helps governments, charities and international institutions publish and use open data. It works on data standards, governance, infrastructure and the tools that let organisations share information across boundaries.
- [Open Innovations](https://open-innovations.org) - A Leeds-based not-for-profit open-data organisation. It stopped taking new work in May 2026 and its tools, data and blog remain online as an archive.
- [Open Knowledge Foundation](https://okfn.org/en/) - The Open Knowledge Foundation is a not-for-profit organisation that builds digital tools and infrastructure for open data and open knowledge. It runs projects including CKAN, Open Data Day and the Open Data Editor, and supports a global network of open knowledge communities.
- [Open Ownership](https://www.openownership.org/en/) - A non-profit that helps governments publish and use high-quality data about who owns companies and other legal entities. It promotes beneficial ownership transparency to reduce corruption and tax evasion and supports the global Open Ownership data standard.
- [OpenUK](https://openuk.uk) - A not-for-profit convening UK business, academia and technologists across open source, open hardware, open data, open standards and AI openness.

### Environmental citizen science

- [Network for Clean Air](https://www.cleanairuk.org/) - A UK network that brings people and communities together to improve air quality. It organises a citizen science programme to measure air pollution.
- [The Polar Citizen Science Collective](https://polarcollective.org/) - A registered charity that unites polar tour operators, scientists, guides and travellers to collect standardised data for research. It runs citizen science projects and a training platform for the polar tourism industry.
- [Then Try This](https://thentrythis.org/) - A non-profit research, design and development organisation in Cornwall. It builds open source software, hardware and participatory projects on environmental and social issues, including citizen science and community technology.
- [Water Testing River Thames (WTRT)](https://watertestingriverthames.co.uk/) - Water Testing River Thames is a volunteer group testing water quality along the River Thames from Oxford to Teddington. It publishes E. Coli and intestinal enterococci results as open data.

## Civic and community

### Civic tech

- [38 Degrees](https://home.38degrees.org.uk/) - A UK campaigning organisation whose members start and sign petitions and take action on political and social issues. It provides campaigning tools and staff support for member-led campaigns.
- [Campaign Lab](https://campaignlab.uk/) - A network of campaigners, data scientists, researchers and technologists who build and test digital tools for progressive political campaigns. It runs hack nights, experiments and research to improve how campaigns use data and technology.
- [Centre for Collective Intelligence](https://www.nesta.org.uk/centre-for-collective-intelligence/) - The Centre for Collective Intelligence is a Nesta project that helps public and voluntary sector organisations involve citizens in decisions on complex issues. It designs digital tools and deliberative engagements, using technology, AI, arts and design to scale public participation.
- [Common Knowledge](https://commonknowledge.coop/) - A not-for-profit worker cooperative of technologists, designers, researchers and facilitators. It builds digital tools and provides training and strategic advice to help grassroots organisers and communities increase their impact.
- [Democracy Club](https://democracyclub.org.uk) - A non-partisan UK community interest company that produces election data, runs WhoCanIVoteFor and WhereDoIVote, and publishes candidate and polling-station data for reuse.
- [Demos](https://demos.co.uk) - An independent educational charity and cross-party think tank working on democracy and public policy. Its Demos Digital work and participatory research use technology to involve citizens in policy making.
- [Diagonal Works](https://diagonal.works/) - A steward-owned data science and visualisation consultancy that builds software tools for understanding urban systems. It uses open data to help planners, public agencies and developers assess how changes to a city may affect people and places.
- [Iswe Foundation](https://www.iswe.org/) - An independent not-for-profit innovation centre working on participatory political systems. It runs Assemblis, an online platform that gives communities tools to run local assemblies.
- [mySociety](https://www.mysociety.org) - A UK charity, established in 2003, that builds and runs digital services such as FixMyStreet, TheyWorkForYou and WhatDoTheyKnow to help people participate in civic life and hold institutions to account.
- [Nesta](https://www.nesta.org.uk/) - A research and innovation foundation that designs, tests and scales solutions to social challenges. Its work includes collective intelligence, data visualisation and policy research.
- [Newspeak House](https://newspeak.house/) - An independent residential college in London that studies and supports communities of practice across civil society and the public sector. It runs a fellowship programme and hosts hackathons, meetups and lectures on political technology and digital infrastructure.
- [Scottish Community Development Centre](https://www.scdc.org.uk/) - The Scottish Community Development Centre is a charity and the national lead body for community development in Scotland. It supports community groups and public bodies with training, research and the VOiCE community engagement software.
- [Sortition Foundation](https://www.sortitionfoundation.org/) - Promotes citizens' assemblies and runs democratic lotteries to select representative groups of people. It uses its Open Democratic Lottery Platform to help governments, councils and community groups organise deliberative processes.
- [TheyWorkForYou](https://www.theyworkforyou.com) - A mySociety civic-tech service that makes Hansard, votes and representatives searchable across the UK's parliaments.

### Government technology

- [CivTech Scotland](https://www.civtech.scot/) - A Scottish Government team that brings the public and private sectors together to build solutions to public sector problems. It runs challenge rounds in which individuals, companies and organisations build new products with public bodies.
- [DG Cities](https://www.dgcities.com) - A council-owned urban innovation company that helps local authorities adopt technology and data. Its projects cover digital inclusion, housing data, transport innovation and tools for meeting decarbonisation targets.
- [Government Digital Service](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/government-digital-service) - The Government Digital Service is the UK government's digital centre, building and setting standards for public services including GOV.UK, GOV.UK One Login and GOV.UK Pay.
- [Justice Digital](https://mojdigital.blog.gov.uk/) - The Ministry of Justice's digital, data and science team. It builds and runs digital services and technology used across the justice system.
- [LocalGov Digital](https://localgov.digital) - A network for digital practitioners working in and around UK local government, with more than 3,000 members. It runs an online community, events and shared resources for councils.
- [MapIt UK](https://mapit.mysociety.org/bulk/) - MapIt is a mySociety service that maps postcodes and locations to UK administrative boundaries. Its bulk tool adds council, constituency and other area columns to uploaded spreadsheets.
- [One Team Gov](https://oneteamgov.uk/) - A global community of public sector reformers who meet online and in person to share practical ways to improve government services. Its UK network runs regular meetups and maintains a Slack community for civil servants, service designers and technologists.
- [Policy Lab](https://openpolicy.blog.gov.uk/) - A UK government team that works to improve policymaking through design, innovation and people-centred approaches. It runs practical projects and provides digital co-design resources for civil servants, local authorities and communities.
- [SocietyWorks](https://www.societyworks.org/) - A not-for-profit social enterprise owned by the civic technology charity mySociety. It builds and runs open source digital services that help local authorities and other public bodies interact with citizens, including FixMyStreet Pro, WasteWorks and FOIWorks.
- [The National Archives](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/) - The UK Government's official archive and publisher for England and Wales. It preserves national records and gives the public online access to catalogues, digital archives and related government information services.

### Nonprofit and NGO technology

- [CAST](https://www.wearecast.org.uk/) - A charity that helps UK charities and social impact organisations use digital and AI. It provides free learning, resources and peer groups to build digital knowledge and confidence.
- [DataKind UK](https://www.datakind.org.uk) - A charity that helps other third sector organisations use data analysis and data science. It matches charities and social enterprises with skilled data volunteers and runs free projects, resources and events.
- [The Developer Society](https://www.dev.ngo/) - A not-for-profit digital co-op that designs, builds and supports websites, digital services, data systems and responsible AI for charities and NGOs. It is a community benefit society run by its employees.

### Crisis and humanitarian technology

- [British Red Cross](https://www.redcross.org.uk/) - The British Red Cross is a humanitarian charity that helps people in the UK and overseas during conflicts, disasters and individual emergencies. It runs emergency response, health and care, first aid and refugee services, supported by in-house technology and digital teams.
- [MapAction](https://mapaction.org/) - A non-profit organisation that provides mapping, geospatial and data expertise during humanitarian emergencies. It works with disaster response agencies to turn incoming data into maps and analysis that support decisions.
- [UK Humanitarian Innovation Hub (UKHIH)](https://devtracker.fcdo.gov.uk/programme/GB-CHC-1177110-UKHIH/summary) - The UK Humanitarian Innovation Hub is a UK-funded programme that brings government, science, private and humanitarian actors together. It supports innovations and technologies for humanitarian responses to disasters and conflict.

### Human rights technology

- [Big Brother Watch](https://bigbrotherwatch.org.uk) - A UK civil liberties campaign group focused on privacy, surveillance, facial recognition and free speech.
- [Center for Countering Digital Harm](https://www.counterharm.com/) - The Center for Countering Digital Harm is a non-profit research and advocacy organisation that investigates digital harm and holds technology companies accountable. It publishes research and runs campaigns for platform regulation and online safety.
- [Centre for Information Resilience](https://www.info-res.org/) - The Centre for Information Resilience is a non-profit social enterprise that uses open-source research and digital investigations to expose disinformation, human rights abuses and war crimes. It works with local partners and media to verify digital evidence from conflict zones.
- [Digital Action](https://digitalaction.co/) - A non-profit network that works with civil society partners to hold technology companies and governments accountable for digital threats to democracy and human rights. It builds coalitions and publishes research on issues including AI, online safety and women's rights online.
- [Foxglove](https://www.foxglove.org.uk/) - A non-profit community interest company that uses litigation, campaigning and communication to challenge abuses by technology companies and governments. Its cases and campaigns examine the use of technology in public services and the power of large technology companies.
- [HateLab](https://hatelab.net/) - A research hub at Cardiff University that studies hate speech and hate crime. It uses data science and AI to measure online harm and supports organisations with tools for identifying threats.
- [Liberty](https://www.libertyhumanrights.org.uk) - A UK civil liberties organisation that campaigns, litigates and advocates on human rights, including privacy, surveillance and free expression.
- [Luminate](https://luminategroup.com/) - A philanthropic foundation that funds organisations working for a fairer digital age. It supports digital rights, privacy, public interest media and technology policy work across Africa, Asia, Europe and Latin America.
- [Open Briefing](https://www.openbriefing.org/) - Provides security and wellbeing support to activists, communities and movements at risk. It helps them strengthen their physical safety, digital resilience and collective wellbeing, and advises nonprofits and foundations on managing risk.
- [Open Rights Group](https://www.openrightsgroup.org) - A UK grassroots digital rights campaigning organisation working to protect privacy and free speech online. It campaigns on surveillance, data protection, AI and the effects of technology on people's rights.
- [Privacy International](https://privacyinternational.org) - A London-based charity working on technology and human rights, with a focus on surveillance and the exploitation of personal data. It has worked on privacy since 1990.
- [Small Media](https://smallmedia.org.uk/) - A non-profit foundation that works to protect freedom of expression and access to information in politically closed societies. It carries out research, advocacy and training, and develops online resources to help human rights defenders work more safely.
- [Team CommUNITY](https://www.digitalrights.community/) - A community network that supports digital rights defenders, civil society groups and journalists facing technology-related threats. It runs community spaces, a wiki, a job board and events to share knowledge and build cross-border solidarity.
- [Whistleblowing International Network](https://whistleblowingnetwork.org/Home) - The Whistleblowing International Network is an international charity based in Scotland that supports civil society organisations defending whistleblowers. It provides tools, expertise and a shared platform, and runs the EU Whistleblowing Monitor to track whistleblowing law across Europe.

### Legal aid and justice technology

- [Citizens Advice](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk) - A national charity giving free, independent advice on benefits, debt, housing, employment, consumer and legal problems through its website and a network of local offices.

### Journalism and media technology

- [BBC Media Action](https://www.bbc.co.uk/mediaaction) - The BBC's international charity. It works with local partners to provide impartial media and information, and trains journalists in countries affected by conflict, disaster and disinformation.
- [BBC Monitoring](https://monitoring.bbc.co.uk/) - A BBC service that reports and analyses news from radio, television, press and online sources around the world. It provides media monitoring and open-source intelligence to subscribers.
- [Full Fact](https://fullfact.org) - An independent UK fact-checking charity that checks claims made online and in public debate, and works with platforms, regulators and researchers to reduce harm from bad information.
- [Global Disinformation Index](https://www.disinformationindex.org/) - The Global Disinformation Index is a not-for-profit research organisation that maps the money behind disinformation and publishes data on how advertising and platforms fund harmful content. Its research is used by policymakers, regulators and industry to act on online harms.

## Access and inclusion

### Digital inclusion

- [Good Things Foundation](https://www.goodthingsfoundation.org) - A UK digital inclusion charity running the National Digital Inclusion Network, National Databank, National Device Bank, Learn My Way and the AI Gateway.
- [Jangala](https://www.janga.la/) - A registered charity that provides internet connectivity equipment for humanitarian emergencies and digitally excluded communities. It supplies its Big Box and Get Box devices to aid agencies, community organisations and public bodies.

### Disability and accessibility technology

- [RNIB](https://www.rnib.org.uk) - The UK's leading sight loss charity, providing practical and emotional support to blind and partially sighted people and campaigning for accessible products and services. It also runs specialist services including Talking Books and RNIB Connect Radio.
- [Scope](https://www.scope.org.uk) - A disability equality charity in England and Wales providing practical information, support, and campaigns on the barriers disabled people face. It runs a helpline, disability energy service, and charity shops.

### Education equity technology

- [Apps for Good](https://www.appsforgood.org) - A UK education charity providing free computing, AI and essential-skills courses for schools. Its new AI for Good 2.0 course has students build AI-powered apps for social impact.
- [Code Club](https://codeclub.org) - A global network of free coding clubs for young people and part of the Raspberry Pi Foundation. It provides projects, training and support for the volunteers who run clubs.
- [Raspberry Pi Foundation](https://www.raspberrypi.org) - A UK charity that helps young people realise their potential through computing and digital technologies, publishing free learning resources and supporting clubs, teachers and Coolest Projects.

## Climate, food and housing

### Climate technology

- [Climate Emergency UK](https://climateemergency.uk/) - Assesses all UK councils on the action they have taken towards net zero and publishes the results as the Council Climate Action Scorecards. It also trains and supports residents, councillors and council staff to campaign for faster local climate action.
- [ENHANCE Cities](https://enhancecities.org/) - This interactive mapping tool from UCL's Centre for Advanced Spatial Analysis visualises open data on transport accessibility, travel behaviour and urban development for towns and cities in England and Wales.
- [Green Web Foundation](https://www.thegreenwebfoundation.org) - A Dutch non-profit working towards a fossil-free internet by 2030. It maintains an open dataset of websites running on green energy and builds tools including CO2.js and carbon.txt.
- [Open Climate Fix](https://openclimatefix.org) - A non-profit building open-source machine-learning forecasts for solar, wind, and cloud movements so electricity grids can use more renewable power. Its GB solar forecast is used by the National Energy System Operator.
- [The Restart Project](https://therestartproject.org) - A people-powered social enterprise running community repair events, Fixing Factories and repair directories. It campaigns for the right to repair.

### Housing and homelessness technology

- [Crisis](https://www.crisis.org.uk) - A national UK charity for people experiencing homelessness, running services and campaigning for the policy changes needed to end homelessness.
- [Homeless Link](https://homelesslink.org.uk) - A national membership charity for organisations working with people who become homeless in England, providing training, consultancy, data and policy advocacy.
- [Shelter England](https://england.shelter.org.uk) - A housing and homelessness charity in England providing advice, legal support and emergency helpline services, and campaigning for housing policy change.

## Work, money and learning

### Worker and platform co-ops

- [CoTech](https://www.coops.tech) - A UK-wide network of worker-owned technology and digital service cooperatives. It was founded at Wortley Hall in 2016 and became the first sector-specific arm of the workers.coop federation in September 2024.

### Financial inclusion and fintech

- [Turn2us](https://www.turn2us.org.uk) - A UK charity helping people facing financial insecurity. It runs a benefits calculator, a charitable grants search and direct grant programmes, and says its roots stretch back to 1897.

### Research and education technology

- [Edinburgh Futures Institute](https://efi.ed.ac.uk/) - A University of Edinburgh institute for interdisciplinary learning, research and innovation. It supports data-intensive research and brings researchers, students, communities and industry together to work on complex social issues.
- [Natural Language Processing Group, University of Sheffield](https://sheffield.ac.uk/cs/research/groups/natural-language-processing) - The Natural Language Processing Group is a research group in the University of Sheffield's School of Computer Science. It is one of the largest language processing research groups in the UK.
- [Open Bioeconomy Lab](https://openbioeconomy.org/) - An interdisciplinary research group at the University of Cambridge that develops open source biomanufacturing tools and reagents. It works with partners in low-resource settings, particularly in the Global South, to make biological research more accessible.
- [OpenSAFELY](https://www.opensafely.org) - A platform for analysing sensitive NHS records safely, built and maintained by the Bennett Institute at the University of Oxford with NHS England, TPP, Optum and collaborators.
- [Oxford Internet Institute](https://www.oii.ox.ac.uk/) - The Oxford Internet Institute is a multidisciplinary teaching and research department at the University of Oxford, dedicated to the social science of the internet. It runs postgraduate programmes and research groups examining how digital technologies affect society, politics and the economy.
- [Teaching Public Service in the Digital Age](https://www.teachingpublicservice.digital/) - Provides free, open-access teaching materials, competency frameworks and training on digital government and AI for public-service educators and government teams. It aims to increase the number of public servants with the skills to lead digital change.
- [The Alan Turing Institute](https://www.turing.ac.uk/) - The UK's independent national institute for data science and artificial intelligence. It works with universities, public bodies and industry on research, skills and policy for data science and AI.
- [Web Science Institute](https://www.southampton.ac.uk/research/institutes-centres/web-science-institute) - The Web Science Institute is a research institute at the University of Southampton that studies web science, data science and artificial intelligence. Its work examines the social challenges and opportunities created by online technologies, including privacy, data and trust in AI.

## Tech ethics and responsible AI

### Technology ethics and responsible AI

- [Ada Lovelace Institute](https://www.adalovelaceinstitute.org/) - The Ada Lovelace Institute is an independent research institute focused on data and AI. It conducts research, policy and practice work to ensure the benefits of data and AI are distributed fairly.
- [AI Security Institute](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/) - The AI Security Institute is a UK government research organisation that tests advanced AI systems and studies their risks. It provides technical evidence to help governments keep the public safe.
- [Cassandra Foundation](https://cassandrafoundation.com/) - An independent policy research organisation studying the economic effects of artificial intelligence and the policies needed for a fair transition. It publishes evidence papers and simulations on AI, work and public services.
- [Citizens' Track on AI](https://citizens-track.org/2026/report/) - The Citizens' Track on AI is an initiative that brings public deliberation and community assemblies into international AI governance. It connects local participatory processes with global governance forums so affected communities can shape decisions about AI.
- [Connected by Data](https://connectedbydata.org/) - A not-for-profit organisation that campaigns for communities to have a powerful say in decisions about data and AI. It runs networks, events and research on participatory data governance.
- [Cooperative AI Foundation](https://www.cooperativeai.com/) - The Cooperative AI Foundation is a charity that supports research to improve cooperation among advanced AI systems for public benefit. It funds researchers and runs workshops and summer schools on cooperative AI.
- [Institute for the Future of Work](https://www.ifow.org/) - The Institute for the Future of Work is an independent research institute studying how new technologies change work and society. It produces research and policy work on responsible AI, automation and good work.
- [Oversight Board](https://www.oversightboard.com/) - The Oversight Board is an independent body that reviews Meta's content moderation decisions on Facebook, Instagram and Threads. It makes binding decisions on difficult cases and publishes policy recommendations on free expression, human rights and platform governance.
- [Worker Info Exchange](https://www.workerinfoexchange.org/) - A non-profit that helps workers reclaim control over data collected from them at work. It uses data access requests, investigations and data trusts to challenge algorithmic management and support legal claims.

### Words used in this list

**API**: a way for one computer program to ask another for data.

**GIS**: software for making and reading maps from data.

**AI**: software that can do tasks that usually need human judgement.
